Last updated: October 7, 2026

We do not collect personal data through this website. No cookies, analytics, or tracking scripts are used. When you authorize our application via TikTok or other platforms (OAuth), we receive only the tokens and scopes you explicitly approve, stored securely and used solely to publish content on your behalf.

2. How We Use Information

Access tokens are used exclusively to: (a) upload content you have approved, and (b) read basic publishing metrics. We never sell, share, or transfer your data to third parties.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

Tokens are retained only while actively used. You may revoke authorization at any time via your TikTok (or other platform) account settings — "Manage app permissions" — after which our access ends immediately. Contact us to request deletion of any stored data.

4. Third-Party Services

Our tools interact with platform APIs (TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Threads) under their own privacy policies.
